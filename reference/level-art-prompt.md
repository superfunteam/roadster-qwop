# Level selection artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using `side.jpeg` and `reference/boosters.png` as car references.

Final game asset: `public/art/test-environments.webp`. Original generated output: `reference/art/test-environments.png`.

Prompt:

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: three panoramic environment thumbnails, a single 1536x1024 image divided into three equal horizontal landscape bands with absolutely no text, no borders.
Primary request: Photorealistic PS3-era premium racing game environment key art for a Tesla rocket Roadster game. Three different locations, realistic physically lit materials, cinematic automotive photography, restrained silver black red color palette.
TOP BAND: spacious premium American home garage seen from doorway, organized aluminum shelves packed with moving boxes and tools, workshop lights, white epoxy floor, afternoon sunlight, silver angular wedge-shaped Roadster in garage.
MIDDLE BAND: empty desert quarter mile drag strip at sunset, extremely long black asphalt lanes vanishing in perspective, starting lights, red-and-white barriers, distant mountains, tiny silver angular sports car at start.
BOTTOM BAND: enormous vertical U-shaped return ramp on a night demonstration ground, black asphalt ramp bending up and overhead into an upside down ceiling lip, red glowing edge lighting, steel trusses, floodlights, small silver wedge sports car with blue downward booster jets near ramp.
Reference images show the exact very angular new silver car: white triangular wedge nose, uninterrupted white LED strip, black low glass canopy, high broad rear wing. Keep those features and avoid the older curvy Tesla Roadster.
Constraints: Three separate composed environments one above another each landscape ratio 4.5:1. No typography, no UI, no graphic logo. High material detail and atmospheric depth.
```
